## Short description

## Long description
**Fling. Merge. Grow a universe.**

Your universe starts with a single warm Sun and a few specks of stardust. Tap empty space to create stardust, then drag it onto its twin. **Two of a kind merge into something new**: pebbles, boulders, asteroids, moons, lava worlds, ocean worlds, garden worlds, gas giants, ringed giants, stars... all the way to a **Black Hole**.

Everything in orbit earns Stardust every second, and bigger bodies earn a lot more. Spend it on upgrades, discover all **17 wonders** in the Codex, and watch your little solar system turn into a living, glowing universe.

- **Physics you can feel:** grab any body, flick it across its orbit or drop it gently into a perfect circle
- **Life evolves:** ocean and garden worlds grow microbes, forests, creatures, glittering cities and finally **Starfarers**, who send you gifts
- **Feed the Sun:** drop spare bodies into your Sun to make it grow and shine brighter for everyone
- **Golden comets:** catch them for Stardust showers, Star Rush (x3), Meteor Gifts and Kinship Storms
- **Wanderers:** lone planets drift past your system. Grab them before they escape!
- **Kinship and Seeder Drones:** twins drift together and merge by themselves, and drones keep seeding your orbits, so your universe grows even while you just watch
- **Supernova:** explode your Sun to be reborn in a new galaxy with a new sky, a new key for the music and permanent **Cosmos perks**
- **Missions, a daily Cosmic Gift and away earnings:** there is always something new to reach for

Every merge plays a note on a gentle pentatonic scale, so combos literally play a melody over a dreamy space lullaby.

## Controls
- **Mouse:** click empty space to create · drag bodies onto their twins to merge · flick to fling · drop on the Sun to feed it · click the Sun for a solar flare · click golden comets
- **Touch:** the same with your finger
- **U** upgrades · **M** missions · **C** codex · **Esc** settings

Works on desktop and mobile, in portrait and landscape.

All art (planets, stars, nebulae, the Sun) is drawn in code and all audio (music and effects) is synthesized in code, with no third-party assets.
