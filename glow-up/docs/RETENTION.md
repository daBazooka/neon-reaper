# Why players stay and come back (and where this game uses it)

These are well-known game-design ideas (my own summary, not data from this game).

| Idea | What it means | Where it is in the game |
|---|---|---|
| **Instant feedback** | Every click must answer back in under 0.1s with sound, motion and a number. | Swing arm, hit sound, floating numbers, camera punch, hammer in hand. |
| **Variable rewards** | Unpredictable prizes are more exciting than fixed ones (slot-machine effect). | 7% **crits** (x5) and 0.5% **jackpots** (x25) on any swing, egg and crate rolls, mutated pickups. |
| **Flow / streaks** | A little skill and rhythm keeps people locked in. | **Mog Streak**: swing without pausing 30 times to trigger **FRENZY** (x2 Aura for 8s, flaming particles). |
| **Goal gradient** | People work harder the closer a bar is to full. | Wide LEVEL bar, Rebirth % on the button, quest bar, hammer prices you can see. |
| **Always one goal in reach** | There must always be a "next thing" within about a minute. | Next hammer, next level, quest, egg, play-time gift countdown. |
| **Collection** | Owning sets and rarities creates "gotta get them all". | 10 hammers, 13 pets with stars, 25 cosmetics, 28-tier looks ladder. |
| **Identity and status** | Showing off who you are beats raw numbers. | Tier tag over every head (Sub 5 to True Adam+), top moggers board, visible pets and hammer. |
| **Escalating power** | Each upgrade should feel bigger than the last. | Hammers x1 to x512, pets up to +1200%, rebirth +50% forever. |
| **Appointments** | Reasons to return at a set time. | Daily streak, Mog Competition every ~2.5 min, server events, play-time gifts (1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 25, 40 min). |
| **Loss aversion** | People hate losing a streak more than they like gaining. | Daily streak, swing streak that breaks if you pause. |
| **Progress while away** | Coming back to a reward feels great. | Offline Aura from equipped items and pets. |
| **Social proof** | Seeing others play makes it feel alive. | Names above heads, duels, competition with bots filling empty spots. |
| **Pacing: easy then hard** | Fast early wins build habit; slower later wins build mastery. | Levels 1 to 50 are quick, then XP cost climbs; rebirth resets the cycle with a bonus. |

## Moments with extra VFX
Level-up and tier-up light pillars and shock rings, FRENZY flames, crit and jackpot bursts, rare pet hatches (Epic and up), play-time gift sparkle.
