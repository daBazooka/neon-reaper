BO KATA — Privacy Policy

The short version

BO KATA does not ask for your personal information, has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

What stays on your device

Your nickname, your look, coins, trophies, kites, spools, settings and quest progress are stored locally on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Online matches

If you switch on ONLINE, the game connects to our game server so you can fly against other players. While you play online, the server receives:

  • the nickname you chose (it is shown to the other players in your match),
  • your kite, thread and spool choices and your trophy count (used to match you with similar players),
  • your in-game inputs while the match runs.

This information is used only to run the match. It is kept in memory for the length of the match and is not saved, sold or shared. Like any internet service, the server necessarily sees your IP address while you are connected; we do not log or store it. Nicknames are filtered for offensive words, and you can hide other players' nicknames in Settings. Offline play sends nothing at all.

Children

BO KATA has no chat, no ads and no purchases, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Online play shares only a nickname for the length of a match. Parents who prefer can leave ONLINE switched off.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only for online matches. It may use vibration for game feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Real-life safety

Kite flying is joyful — please fly safely: never use glass-coated or metal thread, which injures birds and people, and never fly from unsafe rooftops or near power lines.
