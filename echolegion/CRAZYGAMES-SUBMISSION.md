## Long description
**You are the legion.**

A giant clock hand sweeps around the arena. Every time it strikes twelve, the last 5 seconds of your moves come back as an **ECHO**: a ghost of you that repeats every dash, slash and slam, forever. Fight, and you are also choreographing an army of yourself.

Hit an enemy together with one of your echoes for a **SYNC STRIKE** of up to triple damage. Fill your meter and unleash **LEGION**: time stops, and you and every echo teleport across the arena, cutting everything down.

- **Brutal, fast combat:** tap or click to dash-slash, slam the ground, parry bolts with your blade
- **Your past becomes your army:** up to 8 echoes that replay your exact moves
- **SYNC STRIKES and LEGION:** team up with yourself for massive combos
- **7 enemy types:** swarming Shards, Hexers, charging Brutes, Leapers, Splitters, shield-casting Wardens, and **Echo Eaters** that hunt your legion
- **Epic bosses:** the pendulum-swinging **Metronome** and the **Hollow King**, who makes echoes of his own
- **A new power after every wave:** Crescent Wave, Sync Storm, Afterblade, Legion+ and more
- **Hit-stop, shattering kills, a warping neon floor and a darksynth soundtrack**
- **Unlock 7 blades** and permanent upgrades, plus player levels, missions and a daily trial

## Controls
- **Mouse:** move toward the cursor (or use WASD), **left click** to dash-slash, **right click** or **Space** to slam, **E** for LEGION
- **Touch:** drag anywhere to move, **tap** to dash-slash toward that spot, plus **SLAM** and **LEGION** buttons
- **Keyboard only:** WASD to move, **J** to dash-slash, **K** to slam, **L** for LEGION
- **1 / 2 / 3:** pick an upgrade · **P / Esc:** pause

Works on desktop and mobile, in portrait and landscape.
