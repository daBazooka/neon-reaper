----- DESCRIPTION (paste into the Description box) -----

Drift. Burn. Survive.

You're on the run across an endless sun-baked desert, and every bandit, sheriff and biker in the county wants you. You have no guns and no weapons, just a car that sets the ground on fire every time you drift.

Carve burning arcs into the sand and lure your chasers through the flames. Burning cars explode, and explosions set off the cars next to them. Drive a circle around a pack for a massive BURN CHAIN.

• Drift = fire: turn hard and your tires leave a trail of flames
• Chain reactions: one explosion can wreck an entire convoy
• Nitro rams: drifts and near misses charge nitro. Boost to smash cars into scrap
• Rising heat: buggies, sheriffs, outriders, armored rigs, bomb-dropping choppers, and the massive Juggernaut
• Day turns to night: golden afternoon, blazing sunset, then a pitch-black desert lit only by your headlights and the fires you start
• A pit stop at every heat level: Wide Flames, Twin Exhaust, Tumble Mines, Phoenix Engine and more
• Garage: unlock 7 cars (muscle car, dune buggy, pickup, woodie wagon, sports coupe, rally hatch, hot rod) and permanent upgrades, plus missions and a daily run

HOW TO PLAY
• Mouse: your car steers toward the cursor. Turn hard to drift. Hold left button for nitro, right button for the handbrake
• Keyboard: A / D or the arrow keys to steer, Space for the handbrake, Shift / W for nitro
• Touch: drag anywhere to steer, and hold the NITRO and DRIFT buttons
• 1 / 2 / 3: pick a mod · P / Esc: pause

Works on desktop and mobile, in portrait and landscape.

Your progress saves automatically in your browser.
