## Short description
Draw loops of light around sleeping flowers to make them bloom. Every loop paints the night meadow in color and music!

## Long description
**Loop the light. Bloom the night.**

You are a tiny spirit of light. Everywhere you fly, you leave a glowing ribbon. **Cross your own ribbon to close a loop**, and everything inside it wakes up: sleepy buds burst into smiling flowers, and the meadow is painted in soft color where your loop was.

Every bloom plays a note in tune with the music, so the more you loop, the more you play the soundtrack yourself. Catch many flowers in one loop for **HARMONY**, and keep looping fast to build a **glow chain** that raises the melody.

Your light slowly fades, so keep blooming to stay bright. The little **glooms** of the night drift toward you. Don't touch them: loop around them instead to set them free as **butterflies** that fly off and bloom flowers for you.

- **One simple, original idea:** draw a loop, wake up everything inside it
- **Music you make:** every bloom is a note in the current key, and the key changes each night hour
- **A meadow that remembers:** your loops stay painted, and you can see your whole meadow at the end of each run
- **5 kinds of glooms:** sleepy Motes, darting Snippers that snap your ribbon, Chasers, crying Weepers and big Broods
- **The Hush:** a giant sad gloom that rises every fifth hour. Loop around it three times to free it
- **Pick a blessing** at every garden level, like Long Ribbon, Echo Bloom, Wishing Star or Twin Spark
- **Unlock 7 spirits** and permanent upgrades, plus player levels, missions and a daily meadow

## Controls
- **Mouse:** your spirit follows the cursor. Hold the mouse button to boost
- **Touch:** drag anywhere, like a trackpad. Your spirit copies your finger's path. Hold BOOST or a second finger to boost
- **Keyboard:** WASD or the arrow keys to fly, Space or Shift to boost
- **1 / 2 / 3:** pick a blessing
- **P / Esc:** pause

Works on desktop and mobile, in portrait and landscape.
