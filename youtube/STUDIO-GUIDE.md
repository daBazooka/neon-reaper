# From files to live channel: exactly what goes where

Everything happens in **YouTube Studio**: go to **studio.youtube.com** and sign in with the channel's Google account. (Menu names sometimes change slightly. If a label differs, look for the closest one. I can't see your screen.)

You need these files from the chat (or from the repo folders named in brackets):

| File | Where it came from |
|---|---|
| `logo.png` | brand kit [`youtube/brand/`] |
| `banner.jpg` | brand kit |
| `watermark.png` | brand kit |
| `thumbnail-A.jpg`, `-B.jpg`, `-C.jpg` | thumbnails [`youtube/brand/thumbs/`] |
| `bulletin-01-the-glow.mp4` | video [`youtube/videos/dark/`] |
| `short-1` to `short-7` `.mp4` | video |
| Text to copy | `CHANNEL-DESCRIPTION.md` and `UPLOAD-METADATA.md` |

---

## PART 1. Set up the channel (do this once, before the first upload)

Go to **Studio > Customization** (left menu).

### 1a. Profile tab
| Field | Paste or upload |
|---|---|
| **Banner image** > Upload | `banner.jpg` |
| **Picture** > Upload | `logo.png` |
| **Video watermark** > Upload | `watermark.png` (show it "Entire video" or "Custom start time") |
| **Name** | `Bureau of Household Attention` |
| **Handle** | your choice: `@HouseholdAttention` or the first free one from `brand/README.md` |
| **Description** | the **About** text from `CHANNEL-DESCRIPTION.md` (replace `[your email]`) |
| **Contact info > Email** | your business email |

Click **Publish** (top right) after changes.

### 1b. Basic settings
**Studio > Settings > Channel > Basic info**
- **Keywords**: paste the keyword line from `CHANNEL-DESCRIPTION.md`.
- **Country**: your country.

**Studio > Settings > Upload defaults**
- **Category**: Film & Animation. **Language**: English. **Comments**: "Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review".

### 1c. Playlist
**Studio > Content > Playlists > New playlist**: name it `Bulletins`, visibility Public.

## PART 2. Upload the long video (Day 1)

1. Top right of Studio: click **Create** (camera icon) then **Upload videos**.
2. Drag in `bulletin-01-the-glow.mp4`.
3. On the **Details** page, fill in:

| Field on the screen | Paste or choose |
|---|---|
| **Title** | `BULLETIN 01: THE GLOW \| Home Safety Tape (1994)` |
| **Description** | the long video description from `UPLOAD-METADATA.md` (chapters included) |
| **Thumbnail** | **Upload file** > `thumbnail-A.jpg` |
| **Playlists** | choose `Bulletins` |
| **Audience** | **No, it's not made for kids** |
| **Age restriction** | leave the default |
| **Show more** > **Tags** | paste the tag line from `UPLOAD-METADATA.md` |
| **Show more** > **Language** / **Category** | English / Film & Animation |
| **Altered content** question | read the wording, answer per YouTube's definition (normally "No" for this stylised animation) |

4. Click **Next**.
5. **Video elements** page: click **Add** next to **End screen** and place two elements over the two dashed boxes in the last 20 seconds (left: subscribe or the `Bulletins` playlist; right: "Best for viewer"). Click **Next**.
6. **Checks** page: wait for it to finish, then **Next**.
7. **Visibility** page: choose **Schedule**, pick **tomorrow's date at 7:00 pm** (your audience's time zone), then **Schedule**.
   Use the same time every day from now on.

### Test the thumbnails
After it goes live: **Studio > Content**, click the video, then click its thumbnail > **Test & Compare** and upload `thumbnail-B.jpg` and `thumbnail-C.jpg`. YouTube tests them for you.

### Pin the comment (right after it goes live)
Open the video on YouTube, write the pinned comment from `UPLOAD-METADATA.md` in the comment box, post it, click the **three dots (⋮)** next to your comment, then **Pin**.

## PART 3. Upload each Short (one per day)

Repeat these steps for each Short. Use the day order in the table below.

1. **Create > Upload videos**, drag in the Short file.
2. **Title**: the title from the table below.
3. **Description**: the Shorts description from `UPLOAD-METADATA.md` (the same for every Short).
4. **Thumbnail**: skip. Shorts pick a frame automatically.
5. **Playlists**: optional. **Audience**: **No, it's not made for kids**.
6. **Visibility**: **Schedule**, 7:00 pm, that day.
7. After it's live, pin the Shorts comment from `UPLOAD-METADATA.md`.

(Vertical videos under 3 minutes are treated as Shorts automatically.)
