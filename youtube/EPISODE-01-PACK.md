# Episode 1 production pack — "Do AI Note-Takers Really Save 10 Hours a Week?"

Why this first: it sits in the fastest-growing niche (AI tools), it is easy to measure honestly, it is safe (no medical or financial advice), and it produces clear numbers for on-screen receipts.

**Rule: every number below is a placeholder until it is measured.** Do not publish any result you did not actually record.

## 1. Test protocol (so the verdict is real)
- **Claim:** "AI note-taking / meeting-summary tools save you 10 hours a week." (Find the actual viral video making it and note its real view count and title at recording time. Show it on screen only if you have the right to show a short clip or a screenshot for commentary; otherwise describe it.)
- **Tools:** pick 3 popular AI note-taking tools, including at least one free tier. Use versions as they exist on the day, and record versions and dates.
- **Task set:** 10 identical tasks, for example 5 recorded meetings or lectures of 20–30 min each and 5 long articles or docs to summarise.
- **Baseline:** do the same tasks by hand first, timed, so there is a real "before". Keep a stopwatch on screen.
- **Metrics per task:** minutes to a usable summary (including fixing errors), accuracy (score against a checklist of 10 facts you defined *before* running the tools), and hallucinated or missing facts.
- **Verdict rules, fixed in advance:**
  - VERIFIED: median time saved is at least 50% with at most 1 factual error per summary.
  - BUSTED: median time saved is under 15%, or errors make summaries unusable.
  - IT'S COMPLICATED: anything in between, and say which task types work.
- **Weekly total:** scale from your measured per-task savings to a typical week and show the arithmetic. Say clearly what workload it assumes. State the sample size and that it's a small test.
- **Disclosure:** state any sponsorship, affiliate link or free access from a vendor. If none, say so.

## 3. Long-form script (10–12 min) — beat sheet
**0:00–0:06 Cold open (write last, from real footage).** Cut the most surprising real moment, e.g. a tool getting a key fact wrong or saving more time than expected. Voiceover: "[Real surprising result], and that's not the twist."

**0:06–0:22 Claim and bet.** "This video says AI note-takers save ten hours a week. I'm going to time three of them against doing it by hand, on the same ten tasks, and the results will be stamped either way."

**0:22–1:20 Rules.** Show the scoring checklist and the verdict thresholds *before* any results. This makes the verdict credible and is the retention anchor: viewers want to see which threshold gets hit.

**1:20–3:30 Round 1: meetings.** Timer on screen. End the round on a cliffhanger: "Tool B just did something I didn't expect. After the break."

**3:30–6:00 Round 2: long documents.** Show one summary error in detail with a highlighted source line.

**6:00–8:00 Twist round.** Try a harder input (a noisy recording, or a dense technical paper). Show where tools break. Real failures are the most shareable moments.

**8:00–9:30 The math.** Animate the weekly time calculation from measured numbers. Show assumptions.

**9:30–10:30 Verdict.** Stamp animation with the stamp applied per the pre-set rules. One-line recommendation per tool, and "who this is for / not for".

**10:30–10:45 Close.** Scoreboard update ("Verified 0 · Busted 0 · Complicated 1"), then: "Comment the next claim you want tested. The top one is next week." End-screen to the next video or playlist.

## 4. Shorts (film or cut from the same footage)
1. **Verdict-first (35s):** 0–3s stamp slam on the claim + the verdict, 3–30s the single most surprising finding with receipts, last frame loops into the first. Caption: "Which AI claim should I test next?"
2. **Failure clip (25s):** the worst real summary error, source line highlighted. Hook: "This AI summary sounds perfect. Watch what it got wrong."
3. **The math (30s):** show the weekly calculation. Hook: "Ten hours a week? Here's the actual arithmetic."
4. **Scoreboard teaser (15s):** Scoreboard counter plus "Next test: [top comment]".
Post Shorts across the week after the long video, not all on the same day.
