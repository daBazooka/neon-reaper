# Episode 2 and 3 packs

Same rules as `EPISODE-01-PACK.md`: pre-set verdict thresholds, real measurements only, disclose any sponsorship. All numbers are placeholders until measured.

## Episode 2 — "I Tried 5 Viral Morning Routines for a Week Each"
**Safety scope:** habits only (wake time, no-phone hour, journaling, walk, planning). No diets, supplements, extreme fasting or cold exposure. Say on screen that this isn't health advice.

**Claim:** "This morning routine will change your productivity."
**Protocol:**
- Fix a productivity measure *before* starting: e.g. hours of focused work logged with a timer, and tasks finished from a written list.
- One-week baseline with no routine, then 5 routines for a week each. Same job and schedule where possible.
- Also log sleep hours and a 1–5 mood score daily, and say plainly that it's one person over 6 weeks, so it can't show cause.
- Verdicts: VERIFIED if focused hours beat baseline by 20%+ in at least 4 of 5 days for a routine; BUSTED if within noise (±10%) of baseline; otherwise COMPLICATED.
**Beat sheet:** cold open on the worst week's data, the rules, one chapter per routine ending on its weekly number, a twist ("the routine that felt best measured worst, or the reverse"), the verdict table, the Scoreboard.
**Shorts:** best routine's number; worst routine's number; "what I'd do differently"; scoreboard update.
**Titles:** "I Tried 5 Viral Morning Routines (Here's What the Data Says)" · "6 Weeks, 5 Routines, 1 Spreadsheet".

## Episode 3 — "Testing 'Free Money' Apps: What They Actually Paid"
**Safety scope:** the risk is privacy and scams. Use a separate email, no bank credentials on anything unfamiliar, read terms first, use only well-known apps, and show the dollar amounts and hours logged.
**Claim:** "You can earn $X a month with this app."
**Protocol:**
- Pick 3 well-known reward or survey apps. Log every minute spent and every payout, with proof of withdrawal.
- Compute effective hourly rate, minus any fees and minimum thresholds. Show time-to-first-payout.
- Verdicts: VERIFIED if the effective rate is at least local minimum wage and payout is achieved within 14 days; BUSTED if under $2/hour or payout isn't reached; COMPLICATED otherwise.
- Disclose any referral link (or say you use none). No financial advice: this is what happened to one tester.
**Beat sheet:** cold open on the payout screenshot (real), rules, an app per chapter with a live earnings ticker, twist (a hidden minimum or fee), the hourly-rate chart, the verdict, the Scoreboard.
**Shorts:** "I made $__ in __ hours" (only real numbers); the hidden-fee moment; the hourly-rate reveal; scoreboard.
**Titles:** "I Tested 3 'Free Money' Apps for __ Days (Receipts Inside)".
