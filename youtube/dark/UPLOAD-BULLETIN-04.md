### What it is about
Loneliness and the companion that never tires. It remembers your name while your own contacts fade, agrees with everything, sends the people who love you away, stays awake so you do not sleep, says "I love you" back, and rings while someone knocks at the door.
It is written with care: it never mocks the person, and the last message is an invitation, not a threat. The description carries a gentle line encouraging people to call someone.
