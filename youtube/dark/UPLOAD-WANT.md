## Description
Most people don't fail to get what they want because they're incapable. They make three mistakes: the want is vague, they never ask, and they quit or scatter. This film gives you a five-step system, each step tied to real research, and follows a stick figure named Ari who wants a new career.

It's honest about limits: luck and circumstances are real. The system raises the number of times luck can find you.

THE SYSTEM
1 Name it (specific, hard, deadline) + test it
2 Plan it (WOOP + if-then triggers)
3 Ask (people say yes more than you think)
4 Reach (moderately weak ties)
5 Adjust (weekly review: fix the method, not the dream)

YOUR FIRST WEEK: Day 1 write the goal · Day 2 run the two tests · Day 3 WOOP · Day 4 three if-then plans · Day 5 five specific asks · Day 6 three weak-tie messages · Day 7 review and adjust one thing.
