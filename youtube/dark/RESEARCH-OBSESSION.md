# Research notes: OBSESSION (sources used for the script; all checked by web search on the build day)

## Psychology
- Vallerand et al. 2003, "Les Passions de l'Ame: On Obsessive and Harmonious Passion" (J. Personality & Social Psychology): dualistic model. Harmonious passion = autonomous internalisation; obsessive passion = controlled internalisation, an uncontrollable urge. https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2003_VallerancBlanchardMageauKoesnterRatelleLeonardGagneMacolais_JPSP.pdf
- Vallerand 2010 (J. Personality), passion for work and burnout, study of 490+ teachers: obsessive passion positively related to burnout, harmonious negatively. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1467-6494.2009.00616.x
- Rigid persistence: obsessively passionate cyclists kept riding in dangerous winter conditions more than harmoniously passionate ones. https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2008_Vallerand_CanPsych.pdf
- Clinical OCD is different: intrusive, unwanted, distressing obsessions, time-consuming (often 1+ hour a day) and impairing. NIMH: https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/obsessive-compulsive-disorder-ocd
- Etymology: obsession from Latin obsessio, "a besieging", from obsidere ("to sit opposite, to besiege").

## Neuroscience
- Berridge & Robinson: "wanting" (incentive salience, dopamine-dependent, large robust systems) is separable from "liking" (pleasure, smaller fragile systems, not dependent on most dopamine). https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/berridge-lab/wp-content/uploads/sites/743/2019/10/2016-Berridge-Robinson-Liking-wanting-IS-theory-of-addiction-Am-Psychol.pdf
- Howe et al. 2013, Nature: striatal dopamine ramps up over seconds as rats approach a distant reward, scaling with distance and size. https://www.nature.com/articles/nature12475
- Fisher et al. 2005 fMRI (VTA activation viewing a beloved); Marazziti et al. 1999: serotonin transporter density in new lovers similar to OCD patients.
- Zeigarnik effect: a 2025 meta-analysis (Ghibellini & Meier, Humanities & Social Sciences Communications) found no reliable memory advantage for interrupted tasks; the urge to resume (Ovsiankina effect) is what holds up. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-05000-w
- Flow (Csikszentmihalyi): clear goals, immediate feedback, challenge-skill balance.

## Expertise and effort
- Ericsson 1993: the 10,000 hours figure was an average for top violin students, not a threshold; Ericsson later called the "rule" a misinterpretation. Macnamara et al. 2014 meta-analysis: deliberate practice explained about 26% (games), 21% (music), 18% (sports) of performance variance. https://scottbarrykaufman.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/Macnamara-et-al.-2014.pdf

## People
- Andrew Wiles: seven years working on Fermat's Last Theorem in his attic office; announced 23 June 1993; error found September 1993; fixed 19 September 1994; published 1995 (Annals of Mathematics). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiles's_proof_of_Fermat's_Last_Theorem
- James Dyson: 5,127 prototypes over about five years (1978 to 1983) before the G-Force vacuum. https://www.dyson.co.uk/discover/archive/2021/relentless-engineering
- Jiro Ono / Jiro Dreams of Sushi (2011): apprentices train about ten years before cooking the egg sushi; Daisuke Nakazawa describes more than 200 rejected tamagoyaki. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiro_Ono
- Kobe Bryant: 4 a.m. training; "That's where obsession comes in... when you care about something 24 hours a day"; Team USA trainer story: 4:15 a.m. to 11 a.m. until 800 makes. https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/everybody-elses-alarm-clock-just-152300918.html
- Darwin: about eight years (1846 to 1854) on barnacles (four-volume Cirripedia monograph) before On the Origin of Species (1859). https://www.darwinproject.ac.uk/commentary/life-sciences/darwin-and-barnacles
- Marie Curie: notebooks still radioactive (radium-226, half-life about 1,600 years), kept in lead-lined boxes; she died of aplastic anaemia. https://www.sciencealert.com/these-personal-effects-of-marie-curie-will-be-radioactive-for-another-1-500-years
- Michael Jordan, The Last Dance (2020): "I don't have a gambling problem, I have a competition problem"; the Atlantic City trip before Game 2 vs the Knicks, 12-for-32 in a 96-91 loss. https://www.nbcsports.com/nba/news/michael-jordan-denies-gambling-problem-during-last-dance
- Grigori Perelman: proved the Poincare conjecture; declined the 2006 Fields Medal and the 2010 Millennium Prize ($1M). https://www.science.org/content/article/thanks-no-thanks
- Captain Ahab (Moby-Dick, Melville, 1851): monomania after the whale took his leg; the obsession consumes the crew and the ship.
