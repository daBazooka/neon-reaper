## Fact-check notes (where sources differ; the film hedges these)
- MKUltra: surviving document counts vary (roughly 16,000–20,000 cited). Church Committee said full scope is unknowable.
- Operation Northwoods: memo dated 13 March 1962, declassified 1997; rejected, never carried out.
- Tonkin: NSA historian Robert Hanyok's study, declassified 2005, concluded no second attack on 4 Aug 1964.
- Iran–Contra: conviction/pardon counts vary by source; several convictions were vacated on appeal; pardons Dec 1992.
- Poisoned alcohol: death estimates (≥10,000) are disputed, as is intent.
- Business Plot, CIA & the press ("Mockingbird" name is from a later book), Gladio terror links: marked partly confirmed / disputed.
- Post Office: 39 convictions quashed April 2021; 2024 Act quashes qualifying convictions; "900+ wrongly convicted, ~700 by Post Office" is an estimate.
- DuPont, Radium Girls, Phoebus cartel, LIBOR figures: use "reported/about" wording, as in the film.
- Verify before relying: dates/amounts quoted from memory of the sources below.
