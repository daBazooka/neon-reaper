## Description
Obsession can out-work discipline and out-learn consistency. It can also quietly cost you your sleep, your body, your people and your sense of who you are. Meet Sam, a stick figure who pays all four prices, and learn the five-rule leash that keeps the fire without the burn.

IMPORTANT: this film talks about depression and mention of suicidal thoughts (Michael Phelps's own public account). If any of it feels close to home, talk to someone you trust. In the US you can call or text 988; elsewhere, use your local crisis line. OCD is a treatable medical condition; if intrusive, distressing thoughts take over your day, talk to a doctor or therapist.

Illustrative visuals (Sam's curve, hours bars) are not data. The Concorde example is the classic "sunk cost" illustration, not a claim about any one decision.
