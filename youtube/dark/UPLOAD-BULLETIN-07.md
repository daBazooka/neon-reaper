## What the viewer gets (why they come back)
Every bulletin from now on has one thing you can use. This one teaches you to find your daily screen-time number, turn it into days per year, see what else that time could buy, and apply five settings that work. It ends on a screenshot-able summary card.

| Part | Real content |
|---|---|
| Hook | 4 h/day = 1,460 h/year = 61 full days |
| Your daily budget | 24-hour donut: sleep 8, school/work 8, meals and chores 3, screens 4, free 1 (an example day) |
| Rule 1: Know your number | Where to find it: **iPhone: Settings → Screen Time. Android: Settings → Digital Wellbeing** |
| Rule 2: Do the math | Table: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 h/day → 15.2, 30.4, 45.6, 60.8, 91.3 days/year → 5, 10, 15, 20, 30 months per decade. Formula: hours × 365 ÷ 24 |
| Rule 3: Spend it on something | 1,460 h is about 240 books (at ~6 h each), about 2 new languages (FSI estimates ~600–750 class hours for the easiest languages for English speakers), about 7,300 km on foot (at 5 km/h). Shown on screen as illustrative estimates |
| Rule 4: Free is a price | The "free store": every free item costs about 4 hours a day |
| Five tools | 1 mute alerts that are not from people · 2 charge the phone outside the bedroom · 3 pause 10 seconds before opening · 4 20-20-20 for your eyes (every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds) · 5 stop at a time, not at the end of the feed |
| Redrawn day | Same 24 hours: screens 4→2 h, plus a 1 h walk and 1 h reading |
| Summary | Screenshot card of the 4 rules and 5 tools, and "comment your number" |

General-information note: this is not medical advice. The description says so.
