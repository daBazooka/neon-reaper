### What it is about
The first four bulletins were the Glow's rules. This one is the Keeper's answer: eight rules for getting out, each a small real act (open a window, keep a second chair, remember a name, leave a light on, keep the phone close, watch a sunset, listen, look up). The bulletin ends by telling you to stop watching, and cancels its own autoplay.
