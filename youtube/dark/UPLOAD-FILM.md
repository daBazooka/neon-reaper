## Description
An animated thought experiment, not a prediction. At 7:42 on an ordinary Tuesday, every woman and girl on Earth disappears. The film follows the cities, hospitals, schools, homes, farms and factories as the world tries to cope, using real figures from WHO, ILO, UNESCO and FAO.

It is not about men being unable to do these jobs. They could learn them. It is about scale and speed, and how much of daily life rests on women, often unpaid and unseen.

Statistics used (see sources): about 70% of health and social care workers are women (24M of 28.5M nurses and midwives); about 94% of pre-primary teachers; 16.4 billion hours of unpaid care work per day, 76% by women; about 43% of the farm workforce in developing countries; about 2 in 5 of the world's workers; no technology can carry a human pregnancy outside a body. The ageing and population decline after year 10 is an illustrative sketch, not a forecast model.
