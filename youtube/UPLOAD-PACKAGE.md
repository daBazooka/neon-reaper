## Why it's built this way (from `PLAYBOOK.md`)
- **Hook in the first second:** each skit opens on a premise line ("POV: you tried…") and an immediate visual gag.
- **Escalation and reversals:** every skit ends on a punchline plus a stamped verdict.
- **Open loops and repeat value:** a Scoreboard counter runs in the top strip and carries across the long episode; recurring gags (the stamp, "BED", the moths).
- **Participation:** every video ends by asking for the next hack.
- **Music follows the scene:** suspense, chiptune chase, lo-fi, funk, sad trombone and upbeat, switching on the beat of each scene change, with a sound effect on every punchline.
