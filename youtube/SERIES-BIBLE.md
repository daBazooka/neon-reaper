# Series: REAPER RUN — "100 Waves. 1 Life. You Choose the Curse."

## Concept
A hardcore challenge series played in our own browser games, starting with Neon Reaper. One life, 100 waves. Every time the run gets too easy or a wave is cleared, **the comments choose a curse** that makes the next episode worse (for example: "no dash", "enemies explode into more enemies", "screen flips every 20s").

Why this fits the research:
- **Legible mechanism** in the first 5s: one life, 100 waves, a curse chosen by viewers.
- **Loss aversion:** a permanent on-screen streak counter ("WAVE 47 / 100").
- **Participation loop:** comments drive the next episode, which brings viewers back to comment, which lifts engagement.
- **Session contribution:** every episode ends by teeing up the next one, with a curse vote from the previous one.
- **Owned funnel:** the description links to the playable game, so viewers can try to beat the run themselves. Post the best community clears.
- **Cheap to produce:** screen capture plus voiceover or captions. No face needed.

## Long-form template (8–12 min)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Cold open: the most insane moment from later in the video, freeze-frame on a 1 HP bar, then hard cut. |
| 0:05–0:20 | Mechanism: "One life. 100 waves. Chat picks the curses. Let's see how far I get." |
| 0:20–3:00 | Waves 1–30, fast, with the first curse revealed at wave 10 (first mini-payoff). |
| 3:00–6:00 | Waves 30–60, curses stack, near-deaths, a mid-video twist (a boss or a viewer-submitted curse). |
| 6:00–9:00 | Waves 60–90, the streak is now at risk. Slow down at the tense parts, cut the dull ones. |
| Final | Either the clear or the death. Pay off the opening freeze-frame. |
| Last 15s | "The top comment becomes next episode's curse. Vote below." Then the end-screen to the next episode. |

## Shorts template (15–35s, cut from every episode)
1. **"Impossible clutch":** the best near-death, starting on the last 2s of tension.
2. **"Curse reveal":** show the curse, then 10s of the chaos it causes.
3. **"Rank the curses":** numbered ranking of the worst curses, with clips.
4. **"Death cam":** the run ending, with the wave counter. End on the first frame of the loop.
Each Short ends with a question for comments ("Which curse next?") and links to the long episode.

## Sample cold-open script (Episode 1)
> [Freeze-frame: 1 HP, red screen, a wall of enemies.]
> "I have one life. Ninety-nine waves left. And *you* get to make it worse. This is wave 61... and I'm about to lose everything." [hard cut]
> "Rewind. One life. One hundred waves. Every ten waves, one curse. Let's go."

## Metrics to track per upload
- Average % viewed and the drop-off at 0:30.
- Comments per 1,000 views (a proxy for participation).
- Share of viewers who go on to watch a second video (session contribution).
- Click-through rate (test three thumbnails).
- Shorts: viewed-vs-swiped-away rate and loop rewatches.
Rule of thumb: if 30s retention is under about 70%, rewrite the hook before changing anything else.
